Partitioning of Data is useful when we have a huge Dataset and we need to keep some information stipulated to the year month so that we can easily get the information based on the year and month. This prevents reading million or billion of rows from the Dataset.This process is called partition pruning, and it helps improve query performance and reduce unnecessary data processing.

For example, consider a sales dataset containing millions of records collected over several years. We can partition the data based on the year and month columns. If we want to retrieve sales details for January 2025, the system can read only the partition corresponding to year = 2025 and month = 1, rather than scanning sales records from every year and month.
